What We Do

We handle every step.

From the first survey of the land to the moment you turn your key, TAF manages the entire journey of home ownership and property investment.

01 · Development

Real Estate Development

We plan and deliver residential, commercial and mixed-use communities. Every development is designed around how families actually live, with schools, markets, green space and infrastructure included from day one.

  • Master-planned residential estates
  • Commercial & mixed-use districts
  • Smart-city infrastructure planning
02 · Construction

Construction & Engineering

We started life as a construction company and building is still the heart of what we do. Thirty years of site experience show in the materials we choose, the supervision we insist on and the handover dates we keep.

  • Residential & commercial construction
  • Civil & infrastructure works
  • Quality assurance at every milestone
03 · Management

Property Management

Our relationship does not end at handover. We maintain estates, manage rentals and protect the long-term value of every community we build, so your investment keeps working for you.

  • Estate & facilities management
  • Rental & tenancy services
  • Maintenance & value protection
04 · Investment

Investment Consultancy

Africa's property markets reward informed investors. Our consultancy team guides individuals, institutions and governments through land acquisition, portfolio strategy and public-private housing partnerships.

  • Market entry & land acquisition
  • Portfolio strategy & advisory
  • Public-private housing partnerships
05 · Diaspora

Diaspora Home-Ownership

Buying a home from abroad should not require a leap of faith. Our diaspora programme offers verified titles, clear payment milestones and regular progress reports from reservation to key handover, wherever you live.

  • Remote purchase, fully documented
  • Flexible milestone payment plans
  • Photo & video progress updates
