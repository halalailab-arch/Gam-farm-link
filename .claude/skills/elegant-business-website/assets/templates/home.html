A Legacy of Trust

Africa's most trusted name in quality, affordable housing.

TAF started out in 1990 as TAF Construction, a building firm in The Gambia. Today the company operates in eight African countries, and the mission has never changed: to make home ownership a reality for every African family, at home and in the diaspora.

Whether it is a master-planned city or a quiet garden estate, we build to last and we hand over when we said we would. That is the reputation our name carries.
