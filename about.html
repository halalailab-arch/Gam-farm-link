Our Story

Three decades of building trust.

TAF Africa Global was born from a simple belief: every African family deserves a quality home. That belief has carried us from a single construction firm in Banjul to eight countries across the continent.

The Journey

From TAF Construction to TAF Africa Global.

1990

The foundation

Mustapha Njie founds TAF Construction Company Ltd in The Gambia, laying the first bricks of a legacy built on quality and timely delivery.

1990s to 2000s

Setting the benchmark

Landmark communities such as Brufut Gardens Estate redefine residential living in The Gambia and establish TAF as the nation's premier developer.

2017

A Pan-African rebirth

The company becomes TAF Africa Global Ltd, expanding across West, Central, East and Southern Africa with active operations in Nigeria.

2018

The million-homes vision

TAF makes its boldest commitment yet: one million quality, affordable homes for African families by 2038. The same year, ground breaks on Dalaba Estate.

Today

Building tomorrow's cities

With TAF City, a master-planned smart city in Yundum, the company is shaping how a new generation of Africans will live, work and thrive.

2038 1,000,000 homes vision
What Drives Us

Mission, vision & values.

01 Mission

To deliver durable, affordable, quality homes on time and to the highest standard, while treating every buyer with the dignity they deserve.

02 Vision

One million quality affordable homes for African families between 2018 and 2038, changing what home ownership looks like across the continent and the diaspora.

03 Values

We keep our word, we build properly and we deliver when we said we would. Everything else follows from that.

Mustapha Njie at the Africa Housing Awards 2025

Leadership

Mustapha Njie

Founder & Chief Executive Officer

For over thirty years, Mustapha Njie has championed a single idea: Africa's growth begins at home. Under his leadership, TAF has been recognised among the continent's foremost private developers and has earned the trust of families, governments and investors alike.

His work through the African Union for Housing Finance and partnerships across the continent continues to shape policy and practice in affordable housing for millions.
